About

The Story of Legend

Movie short info

Kantara is a powerful action-drama deeply rooted in the traditions, culture, and beliefs of rural Karnataka. Set in a village surrounded by dense forests, the story follows a man caught between modern life and ancient customs passed down through generations.

As long-forgotten traditions resurface, faith, justice, and nature collide, leading to a gripping conflict that challenges power, belief, and identity.

  • Genre: Action / Drama / Thriller
  • Language: Kannada
  • Duration: 2h 28min
  • Release Date: 30 September 2022
  • Director: Rishab Shetty
  • Producer: Vijay Kiragandur
  • Production House: Hombale Films
